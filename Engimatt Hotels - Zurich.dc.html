About us

United by a love for nature and hospitality, we bring guests together for unforgettable experiences in our green oasis, a family-run retreat in the heart of Zurich since 1936

Martin Gehrig Hotel Owner and Founder

Our mission is to offer a place where every guest feels at home: a garden retreat in Zurich, run by the same family for over 90 years.

What we offer

Stay in the garden

Every room opens to its own private balcony over the garden. A quiet green retreat, minutes from Bahnhofstrasse.

Leafs seasonal restaurant

Seasonal cuisine in the winter garden and al fresco on the terrace, open to hotel guests and Zurich locals alike.

Business & events

Modern meeting rooms in a quiet garden setting, plus weddings, banquets, and celebrations staged by our chef.
